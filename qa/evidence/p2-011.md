## Focused negative paths

- Initiative slots resolve current life state from live `combatants`, keyed by `instanceId`.
- Dead player, companion, and enemy slots are skipped without rerolling initiative.
- A dead current slot defensively advances or resolves victory/defeat; no active phase without an action path.
- Enemy AI is scheduled at most once per unit per round after live-state rerenders.
- Player dead with a living companion is not defeat; all friendly units dead is defeat.
- 4v4 multi-death live-slot traversal is covered by focused tests.
